A Century of Sound

From a small workshop in Kalamazoo to stages across the world — the story of Gibson is the story of modern music.

1902

The Beginning

Founded in Kalamazoo, Michigan by Orville Gibson — crafting mandolins that would redefine acoustic instrument design.

1936

The First Electric

The ES-150 — the first commercially successful electric Spanish guitar. A revolution in sound.

1952

Birth of a Legend

The Les Paul Model arrives. Solid body, gold top, P-90 pickups. Rock and roll would never sound the same.

1961

The SG Era

Sleek, lightweight, and fierce. The SG becomes the weapon of choice for a new generation of rock guitarists.

Today

Still Handcrafted

Over 120 years later, every Gibson is still built in the USA, still touched by human hands, still made to be played.

Legends of the Classical Guitar

The virtuosos who transformed the guitar from a folk instrument into a concert hall powerhouse.

1893 — 1987
Spain

Andrés Segovia

1893 — 1987 · Spain

The father of the modern classical guitar. He elevated the instrument from a folk curiosity to a respected concert hall soloist.

The Maestro
1933 — 2020
England

Julian Bream

1933 — 2020 · England

A champion of British composers, Bream commissioned works from Britten, Walton, and Arnold, expanding the guitar's repertoire.

The Innovator
b. 1941
Australia

John Williams

b. 1941 · Australia

Renowned for pinpoint accuracy and articulation, Williams bridged classical and popular music with his group Sky.

The Virtuoso
1852 — 1909
Spain

Francisco Tárrega

1852 — 1909 · Spain

The father of modern guitar technique. He composed "Recuerdos de la Alhambra" and shaped the way the guitar is played today.

The Pioneer
b. 1947
USA

Christopher Parkening

b. 1947 · USA

Segovia's protégé and America's reigning classical guitarist. He founded the guitar department at USC.

The Heir
b. 1977
China

Xuefei Yang

b. 1977 · China

The first Chinese classical guitarist to enter a music school in her country. She has expanded the guitar repertoire with Chinese works.
