A Century of Sound

From a small workshop in Kalamazoo to stages across the world — the story of Gibson is the story of modern music.

1902

The Beginning

Founded in Kalamazoo, Michigan by Orville Gibson — crafting mandolins that would redefine acoustic instrument design.

1936

The First Electric

The ES-150 — the first commercially successful electric Spanish guitar. A revolution in sound.

1952

Birth of a Legend

The Les Paul Model arrives. Solid body, gold top, P-90 pickups. Rock and roll would never sound the same.

1961

The SG Era

Sleek, lightweight, and fierce. The SG becomes the weapon of choice for a new generation of rock guitarists.

Today

Still Handcrafted

Over 120 years later, every Gibson is still built in the USA, still touched by human hands, still made to be played.

Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know about our instruments, our craft, and our legacy.

What's the difference between a Les Paul and an SG?
The Les Paul has a thicker mahogany body with a maple top — producing a warmer, richer tone with more sustain. The SG has a thinner, all-mahogany body with a double-cutaway — lighter, faster to play, with a brighter, snappier attack. Both are legendary, just different tools for different tones.
Which guitar is best for a beginner?
For absolute beginners, we recommend the Studio Session Pro — it's lightweight, versatile, and forgiving. If you prefer acoustic, the J-45 Original is a superb starting point with a balanced voice that works for any style.
How should I care for my guitar?
Keep it in a stable humidity (45-55%), wipe it with a soft cloth after playing, change strings every 4-6 weeks with regular play, and store it in a hardshell case when not in use. Our Hardshell Case is designed specifically for this.
What does the Gibson warranty cover?
Every Gibson comes with a limited lifetime warranty covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. Custom Shop instruments include additional coverage. Register your instrument online to activate full protection.
Are Gibson guitars still made in the USA?
Yes — every Gibson instrument is still handcrafted in our Nashville and Bozeman facilities. We're one of the last major guitar makers to keep full production in the United States, with over 300 hands touching each instrument.
Can I customize my guitar order?
Absolutely. Through our Custom Shop, you can choose woods, finishes, pickups, neck profiles, inlays, and hardware. Custom orders typically take 6-9 months. Contact us to begin your build.
